How Square & Stripe power the build

These are the working connections the hub is built on. Square is the operations backbone; Stripe is the payments connection alongside it.

Square

Our service inventory and operations backbone — the connection that has carried the business.
Core connection
  • Service inventory — Square keeps the inventory of services: the nine service lines and their rates live there.
  • Invoice delivery — estimates and invoices are delivered through Square, like Estimate #100123.
  • Booking management — bookings are managed against the Square record.
  • Customer service — customer records and service history run through Square.
  • Merchant management — merchant services, payments, and subscriptions run through Square.
  • Square Appointments — hooks connect Square Appointments to the hub backend, so scheduled calls sync with the booking loop.

Why it matters: the hub doesn't replace Square — it plugs straight into it, so the inventory, invoices, and merchant operation operators already trust stay exactly where they are.

Stripe

The payments connection alongside Square — the second rail for taking and moving money through the hub.
Payments
  • Card payments — service calls and job payments taken through Stripe alongside Square.
  • Online checkout — owners pay from the booking, wherever the property is.
  • Payouts — pros and merchants are paid out through the payments flow the hub settles.
  • One record — Stripe payments reconcile into the same booking and estimate record as Square.

How the two fit: Square holds the inventory and runs the operation; Stripe extends the payment options beside it. The hub is where both meet the job — one booking, one estimate, one payment record, whichever rail the payment rides.
